# Destination photography

The website serves local, optimised WebP files. Photographs illustrate destinations, not Ayubowan staff, customers or guaranteed admission. The experience images listed below use the [Unsplash License](https://unsplash.com/license), which permits commercial website use.

| Public asset | Photographer and source | Treatment |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `images/camp-nou.webp` | [Dmitry Tomashek on Unsplash](https://unsplash.com/photos/people-watching-football-game-during-daytime-pH7_hVJ65ss) | Centre crop of a photograph published in 2020, converted to WebP. The experience page explicitly identifies it as Camp Nou before redevelopment. |
| `images/gothic-quarter.webp` | [Walter Martin on Unsplash](https://unsplash.com/photos/gothic-barcelona-cathedral-towering-over-a-bustling-city-square-B55Mvns1xjs) | Barcelona Cathedral, resized and converted to WebP. |
| `images/barcelona-nightlife.webp` | [Alp Ancel on Unsplash](https://unsplash.com/photos/a-city-at-night-s1E8gYn8guw) | Barcelona skyline at night, resized and converted to WebP. It does not represent a particular venue or promise a viewpoint visit. |
| `images/airport-arrival.webp` | [Iván Sanchez Jimenez on Unsplash](https://unsplash.com/photos/a-couple-of-people-that-are-walking-with-luggage-ieYFuzxuvXU) | Travellers with suitcases at Barcelona–El Prat, cropped around the travellers and luggage, resized and converted to WebP. Replaces the shared Gothic Quarter photograph for Airport Welcome. |
| `images/sri-lankan-food.webp` | [Zoshua Colah on Unsplash](https://unsplash.com/photos/a-plate-of-rice-and-various-south-asian-dishes-_WQ1qARALg4) | Sri Lankan rice and curry photographed in Mirissa, Sri Lanka. Resized/cropped to 1400×900 WebP. The food guide credits the photographer and clearly labels this as a cuisine illustration, not a photograph of either listed Barcelona restaurant. |

Source downloads and unused image candidates are retained in `assets/source/`, outside the public release. Photographer credit links appear on the new experience pages. The older Barcelona, Montserrat, Costa Brava, Gothic Quarter and paella photographs are documented in [the original asset record](../website/ASSETS.md).
